HelpBot FAQ

Explain everything clearly and briefly.

Q: What is HelpBot?
A: HelpBot is a desktop AI assistant created by Dev to help users with chat, voice, and system tasks.

Q: Who created HelpBot?
A: HelpBot was developed by Dev.

Q: Does HelpBot support voice input?
A: Yes, HelpBot supports voice input and text-to-speech.

Q: How do I enable dark mode?
A: Press Ctrl + D to toggle dark mode.

Q: Where is chat history stored?
A: Chat history is stored locally in a JSON file.

Q: Can I export chat history?
A: Yes, chat history can be exported as PDF, TXT, or JSON.
